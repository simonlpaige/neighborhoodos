Your neighborhood is not a people database.

Read the privacy and safety rules
  • No resident profiles or people scores.
  • No predictive policing or monitoring organizers.
  • No automated decisions about who gets help.
  • No household complaint locations in the published dashboard.
  • Public-meeting notes come from human-reviewed sources or the official record.

A few useful answers

Do I need to know technology?

No. Start with the lesson. Most clinic work uses conversation, public sources, and a one-page brief.

Will I be paid to attend?

The cohort budget includes $30 per 90-minute session. A funded session listing must confirm payment before you attend. The online lesson does not include a payment.

Does this replace 311?

No. It helps you find the right source, record the case number, and decide what to do next.
