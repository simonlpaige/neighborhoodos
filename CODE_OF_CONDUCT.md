# Community Conduct

Treat neighbors, staff and contributors with respect. Welcome different levels of technical knowledge, languages, abilities and experience. Explain disagreements with evidence.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing and publication of private resident information are unacceptable. Respect consent; do not record participants without permission.

For program concerns, use [KC Digital Drive's contact channel](https://www.kcdigitaldrive.org/contact/). For site or repository concerns, contact Simon L. Paige at simon@simonlpaige.com. Keep sensitive details out of public issues. The responsible lead or administrator may remove harmful content, limit participation and seek further review.
