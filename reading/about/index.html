Built for neighbors to use.

NeighborhoodOS is a KC Digital Drive program for practical AI learning and civic problem-solving. It starts in Linwood, Kansas City, with a model other neighborhoods can adapt.

Who does what

KC Digital Drive owns the program. Adoption was confirmed in October 2026. Program leadership coordinates sessions, funding, and local participation.

Simon L. Paige created NeighborhoodOS and remains site administrator and advisor. KCDD can run the program without requiring him to lead regular sessions.

Program lead and data steward are separate responsibilities. Named role assignments will be published when confirmed.
