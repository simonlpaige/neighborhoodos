Permit coverage, privacy, and limits

Current city-issued permits are joined by parcel number to the city parcel polygons intersecting the dashboard’s named official neighborhood boundaries. Boundary-crossing parcels can appear in adjacent areas. Only residential and commercial building permits with an issue date are included; electrical, plumbing, mechanical, zoning, and applications without an issue date are excluded. A permit is not proof that work has begun, finished, or is currently authorized. Missing or changed parcel identifiers can prevent a match; zero matched records does not establish no construction.

The city parcel map used for matching was last published Dec 30, 2023. New or changed parcels may be missing. This limitation is separate from the freshness of the permit feed.

No owner names, contractor details, project descriptions, parcel identifiers, exact addresses, coordinates, or raw city rows are published. Recent permit records show a 100-block, public permit number, issue date, category, and recorded status only.

The previous CPD feed (ntw8-aacc) stopped updating in May 2025 and is not used for this section.

Sources: City Issued Permits, city parcel polygons, and official neighborhood boundaries. Download this permit snapshot.

Read this with care.

Resolved and canceled are separate. Closure medians use resolved records with a closure date and nonnegative duration, among requests opened in this window. They exclude still-open requests and do not estimate how long an open request will take. City comparison uses the same opening window and status rules. Unlocated records cannot be assigned to a neighborhood.

No household complaint locations, resident names, free text, coordinates, or individual animal / encampment records are published. Stuck list contains allowlisted public infrastructure only, rounded to a block.

The trend table includes issue counts. Building permits are summarized in their own section. Dangerous-building and Land Bank overlays still await review. Public meetings link to official city records, county records, or Documenters’ human-reported notes.
