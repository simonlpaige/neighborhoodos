Streets, trash, dumping, or a city service

KCMO 311 / myKCMO

Start with the city’s service-request system. Keep the request number so you can check the status or ask why it was closed. Confirm who maintains the location.

Health or safety in a rental home

KCMO Healthy Homes

The Health Department’s rental inspection program receives complaints about health and safety conditions. Check its tenant instructions and current process. For rights or eviction questions, use the city’s tenant resources or a qualified advocate.

Water or sewer service

KC Water

Use KC Water’s service channels for a main break, low pressure, sewer odor, or a service question. Keep account details with the provider. For electricity or gas, use the provider shown on your bill.

Building permits or zoning

KCMO City Planning & Development

The department issues construction permits and supports planning and zoning review. Use its current CompassKC and project-information links. A permit record alone does not tell you whether work is underway.

A Jackson County property valuation

Jackson County Assessment

The Assessment Department values property. Check its current review and appeal instructions. Tax payment questions belong to Collection. Kansas City spans several counties; first confirm which county holds your property record.
