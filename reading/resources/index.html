Find a person or place that can help.

Start with the directories Kansas City already has. They are kept current by their own staff, so this page points to them and does not keep a list of its own.

Start here

  • United Way 211. Food, housing, utilities, health, and most other services. Call 2-1-1 or search 211 online.
  • KCMO 311. City services and reports. Call 3-1-1 or use myKCMO.
  • Neighborhood associations. UMKC Center for Neighborhoods profiles.
  • Small business. KC SourceLink.

These directories are kept current by their own staff. Use them first.
