# Language Access and Translation

The site offers Spanish, Chinese (Simplified), Vietnamese, Arabic, and French as automatic-translation options. Names appear in each language in the Translate menu on every page.

## Why these languages

Kansas City's Language Access Office presentation for Ordinance 240165 identifies these five individually named languages among the city's major language-access needs. Its comparison also includes the collective category “Amharic, Somali or other Afro-Asiatic languages,” plus Punjabi. A collective category is not an individual language; these five choices should not be described as a precise current ranking of all individual languages or of the entire Kansas City metro.

Source: [KCMO Language Access Office presentation, page 7](https://kansascity.legistar.com/View.ashx?GUID=E87B0F6B-CA61-4ADA-9617-EB710966CF9C&ID=12647262&M=F). This is a published city language-access priority source, not a new resident survey. The menu can be expanded as local partners identify needs.

## How translation works

Selecting a language opens Google Translate in a new tab. Google receives the public reading-copy URL and handles the automatic translation under its own terms. No Google translation widget, tracker, or script loads on the original NeighborhoodOS page.

The reading copy excludes editable forms, embedded content, and scripts. It never includes a visitor's answers. Return to the original tab to use the private brief or interactive quizzes. Existing private input remains in that original tab and is not part of the translation URL.

Automatic translation can make mistakes, especially with service rules, dates, legal language, or names. It is not a reviewed translation or a promise of interpreting services. Use the original source and contact KCDD for language help or corrections. The separately published Spanish lesson remains available on the site.
