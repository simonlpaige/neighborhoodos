Read in the language you prefer.

Open the Translate menu at the top of any page. Choose Español, 中文, Tiếng Việt, العربية, or Français.

Automatic translation

Your choice opens a public reading copy in Google Translate in a separate tab. Google handles the translation under its own terms. No Google translation script loads on your original NeighborhoodOS page.

The reading copy has no editable forms and never includes your answers. Return to your original tab to make a private problem brief or use the interactive exercises.

Automatic translations can make mistakes. Check important dates, service rules, and advice against the original source. This is not a reviewed translation or a promise that an organization provides services in that language.

Why these five choices?

These are five individually named languages highlighted in Kansas City, Missouri’s language-access work. The city also groups several other languages together, so this selection is not a precise current ranking of every individual language or the whole metro area.

Read the city source and selection notes. KCDD can add more languages as neighborhood partners identify needs.

Need language help or a correction?

Contact KC Digital Drive with the page and language. Leave out private medical, legal, account, or household details.
