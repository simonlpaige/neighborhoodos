# Evaluation and Metrics

Measure enough to make a decision, not so much that measuring becomes the program. Never collect metrics that identify individuals.

## Day-90 targets (from the pilot design)

| Measure | Target | How to count |
|---|---|---|
| Learn sessions held | 3 | Calendar |
| Solve clinics held | 2 | Calendar |
| Attendance per session | 12-25 | Headcount, not names |
| Returning participants | 30%+ attend 2 or more | Show of hands, or opt-in sign-in |
| Problems captured | 10-20 | Public problem log |
| Problems moved to a concrete next action | 3-5 | Public problem log |
| Build candidate | 1 scoped, or set aside with a written reason | Rubric score sheet |
| Future facilitators | 2+ residents interested | Opt-in list |

## Quick feedback at every session (anonymous, paper or phone)

1. How useful was today? (1-5)
2. One thing you'll do differently this week.
3. What should we change?
4. Would you come back / bring someone? (yes / maybe / no)

## Learning check (optional, anonymous, before Learn 1 and after Learn 3)

Three short scenarios: "An AI tells you the city code section for X. What do you do?" / "You get a call from a family member's voice asking for money. What do you do?" / "Is it safe to paste your lease into a free chatbot?" Compare the share of safe answers before and after.

## The decision memo (two pages, end of pilot)

1. What happened (numbers above)
2. What people said (3-5 quotes, with permission, no names)
3. Problems that moved, and ones that didn't (and why)
4. Safety review: any data collected, any requests over the line, any audit-log blocks
5. Recommendation: continue as is / change X / stop
