Help should be easy to reach.

We aim for WCAG 2.2 Level AA and test the main tasks with keyboard navigation, screen-reader checks, text enlargement, and automated accessibility tools.

Ways to use the site

  • Use the “Skip to main content” link with your keyboard.
  • Zoom or enlarge text; the layout adapts to a narrow screen.
  • Read dashboard charts as tables and print a snapshot.
  • Use the self-paced lesson or its Spanish version without creating an account.
  • Copy or print a problem brief. It stays in the browser tab and is not sent to a server.

Report an access problem

Email the site administrator at simon@simonlpaige.com. Include the page, what you were trying to do, and your browser or assistive technology if you want to share it. Please do not send private health, housing, or account details.

For in-person accommodations, Contact KC Digital Drive before the session. External resource websites are maintained by their own organizations.

WCAG conformance is a testing target, not a claim of legal certification. The release notes describe the checks actually completed.
