A small program needs few people, but the roles must be clear. One person can hold two roles, except program lead and data steward should be different people so there is a check on data decisions.

Role Responsibilities Time (90-day pilot)
Program lead Owns schedule, recruitment, partners, reporting. Final call on scope. 4-6 hrs/week
Lead facilitator Runs sessions, adapts materials, keeps tone right. ~6 hrs per session incl. prep (about 40 hrs total)
Peer helper (co-facilitator) Graduate who helps in the room, gives one-on-one help, and staffs clinics. Paid $20/hour, including prep. ~3 hrs per session
Data steward Approves anything collected, handles deletion requests, reviews the audit log weekly if software is running. See Data Stewardship. 1-2 hrs/week
Technical volunteer (optional until Build) Sets up hardware, runs the software, builds the chosen tool. 0 in Learn/Solve; 20-60 hrs for one Build
Community connectors Trusted neighbors who invite people personally. Often the single biggest factor in turnout. A few hours, ideally with a stipend
Advisory voice 3-5 residents who review plans and flag problems early. 1 hr/month

Paying people

Pay residents for their time: participants ($30 per 90-minute session), peer helpers, community connectors, and advisory members, all at $20/hour, the Kansas City Documenters rate. Stipends change who can participate. The host organization or a fiscal sponsor handles payments. Budget lines and funding are in Budget and Hardware.

Peer helper retention at six months is one of the program's three outcome measures (see Evaluation and Metrics). Paying them, giving them real responsibility, and asking why anyone leaves all help.

Program owner and site administrator

KC Digital Drive owns the program. Simon L. Paige is founder, site administrator, and advisor. He may join kickoff or check-in conversations, but is not the recurring facilitator. KCDD should assign a program lead and a separate data steward; names are not yet supplied. Program inquiries go through KCDD’s official contact page.
