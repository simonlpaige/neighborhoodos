Why these language choices?

The original five choices are individually named languages highlighted in Kansas City, Missouri’s language-access work. Swahili was added to serve local African communities, supported by a recent Census-based Kansas City metro estimate of about 4,300 speakers. The city also groups several other languages together, so this selection is not a precise current ranking of every individual language or the whole metro area.

Read the city source and selection notes. KCDD can add more languages as neighborhood partners identify needs.
