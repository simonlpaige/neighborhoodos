A useful brief is a start. A problem resolved, a better decision, or a neighbor ready to teach is what we want to measure.

No cohort outcomes published yet.

The resources and public-data snapshots are available. The first cohort’s dates, funding, and participant outcomes are not confirmed on this site. No service request in the dashboard is claimed as a NeighborhoodOS result.

What we will report

  • Session participation as aggregate counts, without resident profiles.
  • AI-error spotting before and after learning, using voluntary aggregate checks.
  • Problem briefs with a documented next step, and follow-through checked later.
  • Resolved problems only when a source and human reviewer support that conclusion.
  • Peer-helper retention at six months.

A public problem log will describe places and institutions only, after review. It will not publish household cases, participant names, or sensitive information.
