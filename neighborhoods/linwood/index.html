Turn a stuck issue into a brief

Read this with care.

Resolved and canceled are separate. Closure medians use resolved records with a closure date and nonnegative duration, among requests opened in this window. They exclude still-open requests and do not estimate how long an open request will take. City comparison uses the same opening window and status rules. Unlocated records cannot be assigned to a neighborhood.

No household complaint locations, resident names, free text, coordinates, or individual animal / encampment records are published. Stuck list contains allowlisted public infrastructure only, rounded to a block.

The trend table includes issue counts. Dangerous-building, permit, and Land Bank overlays await a separate review; no household property layer is published here. Public meetings link to official city records, county records, or Documenters’ human-reported notes.
